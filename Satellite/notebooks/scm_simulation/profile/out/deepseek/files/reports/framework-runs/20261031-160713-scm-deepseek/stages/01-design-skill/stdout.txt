**New skill: `skills/statistical-and-machine-learning-foundations-for-science/synthetic-control-att-starter`**

- `scripts/estimate_synthetic_control.R` — fits `dataprep()`+`synth()`, projects the donor-weighted `Y0plot` to the treated periods, computes ATT; supports `--lag-periods`, `--custom-v`, `--genoud`, `--allow-fallback`, auto-discovers a lone `*_panel.csv`, and emits stdout JSON + optional diagnostics.
- `scripts/inspect_panel.py` — panel validation (blocking issues vs. warnings).
- `scripts/make_example_panel.py` — deterministic panel with a known injected effect.
- `scripts/validate_answer.py` — contract and ATT-consistency validator.
