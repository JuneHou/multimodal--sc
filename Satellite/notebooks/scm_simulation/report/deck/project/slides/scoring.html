sectionmeasurecomputed asuses the truth

4.2pre-treatment RMSPEroot mean square of the treated outcome minus the weighted donors over periods 1 to 40no

4.3leave one donor outlargest change in the ATT when one donor with positive weight is left out and the weights refittedno
4.3leave one period outCV-RMSE over periods 1 to 40, each period predicted from outcome weights fitted on the other 39no
4.3held-out blockRMSE over periods 36 to 40 from outcome weights fitted on periods 1 to 35no

Section 4.1 scores each answer against the true untreated path and the true effect. Sections 4.2 and 4.3 use observed data only. The two checks that leave out periods fit weights on the outcome alone and give one value per process, the same for every answer.

Measures of Sections 4.1, 4.2 and 4.3 of the simulation README.

For a configure or design answer, Sections 4.2 and 4.3 use the fit that produced it. For a profile answer they use the skill script’s specification refitted with Synth, which reproduces every answer within 0.0001 in every period.
