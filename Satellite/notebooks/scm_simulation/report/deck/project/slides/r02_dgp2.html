An answer’s ATT is the mean over periods 41 to 60 of the treated unit’s outcome minus the answer’s counterfactual, as the collaborator’s code computes it. ATT bias is its error, ATT RMSE the error’s absolute value, and each within row says whether the error lies within the bound. Random noise is σ√(1 + Σw²) at the answer’s own weights.
