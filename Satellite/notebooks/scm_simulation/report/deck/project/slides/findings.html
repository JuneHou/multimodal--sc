Findings

The formats change who decides the specification

Agreement across models

Under a profile skill the three models give one identical answer, since the script fixes the specification. Under scm‑configure the DGP 1 ATT error runs from 0.164 to 0.326 across models, and under scm‑design both models give the GPT‑5.4 profile skill’s answer.

Reported effects

Three of the four gpt‑oss‑120b answers under scm‑configure and scm‑design state an ATT that differs from the mean gap of its own counterfactual, by 0.38 to 1.58. Every other answer agrees with its counterfactual within 0.0001.

Lowest counterfactual RMSE

On DGP 1 GPT‑5.5 under scm‑configure, both scm‑design answers and the GPT‑5.4 profile skill use 42 predictors, the two covariates and the outcome in each of periods 1 to 40, and reach the lowest counterfactual RMSE, 0.992.

The profile skills remove the model’s choice of specification, and with it both the variation across models and the reporting errors. The GPT‑5.4 generator, GPT‑5.5 under scm‑configure and both models under scm‑design arrived at the same predictor set on DGP 1.
