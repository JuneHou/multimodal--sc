In scm‑configure and scm‑design the solver model picks the predictors, and in a profile skill the generator picked them when it wrote the script. Every answer let Synth search V and kept all 29 donors with convex weights and no penalty.
Predictor sets of the fits behind the σ = 1 answers, grouped. Covariate means are over periods 1 to 40 unless the row or the speaker notes give other periods.
NOTES: Synth searches V in two nested steps. For a candidate V it fits the convex donor weights, then scores that synthetic control by the mean squared error of the treated unit’s outcome over the chosen pre‑treatment periods, and an optimizer changes V to make that error as small as possible. gpt‑oss‑120b under scm‑design on DGP 1 averaged the covariates over periods 1 and 40, which on DGP 1 gives the same values as periods 1 to 40. GLM‑5.3 under scm‑configure averaged them over periods 1 and 20 on DGP 1, and on DGP 2 no recorded fit equals its answer exactly, so the closest recorded fit stands behind it. The optimizers of each V search are listed in the specifications file named on the sources page.
