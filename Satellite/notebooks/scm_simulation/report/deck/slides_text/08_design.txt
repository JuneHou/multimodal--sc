## Everything is yours to decide
`spec.json` holds two kinds of field. The settings are the 13 arguments of Synth's `dataprep` and
`synth` listed in `references/settings.md`. The choices are the paper's alternatives to what Synth fixes
in its code (Abadie, Diamond and Hainmueller …, p. 496):
- `weights`, `convex` (every donor weight at least 0, summing to one, the paper's definition and
Synth's) or `extrapolation` (summing to one, any weight may be below 0 or above one)
- `interpolation_penalty`, `none` or `distance`, which adds `penalty_lambda` times each donor's weight
times its distance from the treated unit's predictors, so that distant donors are kept out
`references/settings.md` defines every field and the rules that tie them. `penalty_lambda` is given
exactly when `interpolation_penalty` is `distance`, and `distance` requires `convex` weights. …
3. Write `spec.json`, then run `Rscript scripts/fit.R spec.json`. It prints the predictors and V, …
Every scm‑design answer kept convex weights without a penalty, the estimator that scm‑configure fixes. The two skills differ in what the model is offered, and the answers used the same estimator under both.
