Data · two simulated panels
Two simulated panels hold a known effect of −3
DGP 1 · shared factors
Y = δt + θt′Zi + λt′μi + εit
Equation 1 of the paper. Every unit loads on four latent factors that all units share, and the covariates Z1 and Z2 stay fixed over time.
DGP 2 · no shared shocks
Yt+1 = 0.5 Yt + β′Zt+1 + ut+1
Equation 5 of the paper. Each unit follows its own autoregression with its own shocks, and the covariates move with the outcome.

Both panels hold 30 units, 1 treated and 29 donors, over 60 periods. Treatment lowers the treated unit’s outcome by 3 in each of periods 41 to 60. Each process was drawn once and comes at three noise levels, its shocks εit and ut scaled by σ = 1, 0.5 and 0.2.
The treated unit’s true untreated path and the README Section 3 synthetic control, periods 1 to 60, at σ = 1. Y1tN is the treated unit’s Y without treatment, as on the method slide.
NOTES: The panels hold observable data only. The ground truth gives the treated unit’s untreated path and the effect in every period, and it is used only to score finished answers.
