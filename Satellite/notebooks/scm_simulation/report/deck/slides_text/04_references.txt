README Section 3
The simulation README’s specification. Z1 and Z2 at period 40 and the outcome at each of periods 1 to 40 make 42 predictors, each scaled by its standard deviation over the 30 units. V is the identity and the convex weights are solved exactly.
ADH specification
The paper’s predictor choices for California, placed at the same points of this pre-treatment window. The predictors are Z1 and Z2 averaged over periods 23 to 40 and the outcome at periods 12, 23 and 40. Synth searches V to fit the outcome over periods 1 to 40.
