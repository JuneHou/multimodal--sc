Method · synthetic control
Synthetic control weights donors to match the treated unit
The effect
α1t = Y1t − Y1tN
The effect in a treated period is the treated unit’s outcome minus its outcome without treatment. Its average over periods 41 to 60 is the ATT.
The donor weights
min (X1 − X0W)′ V (X1 − X0W)
Each weight is at least zero and the weights sum to one. They bring the weighted donors’ predictors X0W as close as possible to the treated unit’s X1.
The predictor weights V
V diagonal, positive
V sets how much each predictor counts. The paper chooses V so that the synthetic control’s outcome tracks the treated unit’s outcome before treatment.
The predictors are the covariates and pre-treatment outcomes, each summarized over chosen periods. After treatment the weighted donors’ outcome is the counterfactual. The method leaves the predictors, V and the donor pool open, and the skill formats differ in who chooses them.
Abadie, Diamond and Hainmueller, Section 2.2 on page 495 and Section 2.3 on page 496.
NOTES: The paper also allows weights below zero or above one at the cost of extrapolation, and a penalty that keeps donors far from the treated unit out of the synthetic control. Both alternatives are open in the scm‑design skill.
