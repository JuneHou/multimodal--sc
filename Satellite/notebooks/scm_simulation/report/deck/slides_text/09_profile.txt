Design a skill for estimating the effect of a treatment on one treated unit from panel data on that unit
and untreated donor units with the synthetic control method of Abadie, Diamond and Hainmueller. …
The paper's own software, the Synth R package version 1.1-10, is at `code/Synth/`, and R 4.3.3 with
Synth and jsonlite installed is available to the agent. … It estimates the treated unit's outcome
without treatment in each treated period the task names and the average effect over those periods, …

GPT‑5.4 generator | synth‑single‑treated‑unit‑scm | Z1 and Z2 means over the pre-treatment periods and the outcome at each pre-treatment period | searched with Synth’s defaults |
