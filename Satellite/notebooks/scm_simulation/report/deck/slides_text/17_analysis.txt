One replication cannot rank the formats
[image: DGP 1 ATT error of the known-factor counterfactual over 11 replications, the two fixed specifications and every answer]
The treated unit’s own noise is part of its true untreated path. The counterfactual that knows every DGP 1 factor misses the effect by the mean of that noise, −0.147 on the shipped panel.
Across 11 replications of the same generator that miss has standard deviation 0.252 and runs from −0.567 to +0.402. Apart from gpt‑oss‑120b, every answer’s DGP 1 ATT error lies between 0.075 and 0.326, inside that range.
DGP 2 shares no shock across units, and over periods 1 to 40 the treated unit’s outcome correlates 0.08 with the donor average. Ranking the formats needs repeated replications compared panel by panel.
