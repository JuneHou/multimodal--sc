Skill formats
The formats differ in who writes the method
format | code the model runs | what the model decides | fixed design |
scm‑configure | a fitter that runs the paper’s code, Synth 1.1-10, unchanged | 13 Synth settings, the predictors and their periods, the donor pool, V or its search, the optimizers and the solver’s precision | convex weights, no penalty |
scm‑design | a fitter that turns four design choices into Synth’s arguments, with a native fit for extrapolation or a penalty | four choices from page 496, the predictors and their periods, how V is chosen, convex or extrapolation weights, and a distance penalty with its size | all 29 donors, Synth’s default optimizers and solver settings |
profile, GPT‑5.4 generator | an R script that GPT‑5.4 wrote through SkillFoundry from the paper and Synth’s source | how to call the script, with an option for the covariate columns | covariates averaged over the pre‑treatment periods, the outcome at each pre‑treatment period, V searched |
profile, DeepSeek generator | an R script that DeepSeek‑V4.1‑Flash wrote the same way | how to call the script, with options for predictors, outcome lags, V and optimizers | convex weights |
Under scm‑configure and scm‑design the model writes the specification itself and runs the fitter at it. Under a profile skill the generator’s script holds the specification, and the model calls the script.
Contents of each skill as the solver models received it.
NOTES: scm‑configure and scm‑design each hold a method reference, a settings reference, a data viewer and the fitter. Each SkillFoundry generator received the same request, the paper and Synth’s CRAN source.
