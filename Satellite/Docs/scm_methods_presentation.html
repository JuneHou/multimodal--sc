Common Notation & Setup

Index Spaces

\[ i \in \{1, 2, \ldots, N\} \text{ units} \] \[ t \in \{1, \ldots, T_0, T_0{+}1, \ldots, T\} \text{ periods} \] \[ k \in \{1, \ldots, K\} \text{ outcomes (bands)} \]
Unit 1 = treated (Hurricane site) Units 2…J+1 = donors (counterfactuals) T₀ = pre-hurricane periods

Core Vectors & Matrices

\[ \mathbf{y}_1 = \begin{pmatrix}Y_{11}\\\vdots\\Y_{1T_0}\end{pmatrix}\!\in\mathbb{R}^{T_0},\quad \mathbf{Y}_0 = \begin{pmatrix}Y_{21}&\cdots&Y_{J{+}1,1}\\\vdots&&\vdots\\Y_{2T_0}&\cdots&Y_{J{+}1,T_0}\end{pmatrix}\!\in\mathbb{R}^{T_0\times J} \]

Weight Vector & Simplex Constraint

\[ \mathbf{w} = \begin{pmatrix}w_1\\\vdots\\w_J\end{pmatrix}\in\mathbb{R}^J \] \[ \mathcal{W} = \Bigl\{\mathbf{w}\in\mathbb{R}^J : w_j \geq 0,\; \textstyle\sum_{j=1}^J w_j = 1\Bigr\} \]

Counterfactual & Effect

\[ \hat{Y}_{1t} = \mathbf{Y}_{0t}^\top\mathbf{w}^* = \sum_{j=1}^J w_j^* Y_{jt} \] \[ \hat{\tau}_t = Y_{1t} - \hat{Y}_{1t}, \quad t > T_0 \]
J = 5–10 donors T₀ = 9–10 biweekly periods K = 8 Sentinel-2 bands

Standard Synthetic Control Method (SCM)

Abadie, Diamond & Hainmueller (2003, 2010)

Optimization Problem

\[ \mathbf{w}^{\text{SCM}} \;=\; \arg\min_{\mathbf{w}\in\mathcal{W}} \; \underbrace{(\mathbf{y}_1 - \mathbf{Y}_0\,\mathbf{w})^\top V\,(\mathbf{y}_1 - \mathbf{Y}_0\,\mathbf{w})}_{\text{weighted pre-treatment imbalance}} \]

where \(V\in\mathbb{R}^{T_0\times T_0}\) is a diagonal positive semi-definite matrix weighting periods.

System of Equations (Quadratic Program)

\[ \underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}\mathbf{Y}_0^\top V\mathbf{Y}_0 \\ \mathbf{1}^\top \\ \mathbf{I}_J\end{pmatrix}}_{\text{constraint matrix } A} \mathbf{w} = \underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}\mathbf{Y}_0^\top V\,\mathbf{y}_1 \\ 1 \\ \mathbf{0}\end{pmatrix}}_{\text{rhs }b} \quad\Rightarrow\quad \min_{\mathbf{w}}\tfrac{1}{2}\mathbf{w}^\top D\,\mathbf{w} - \mathbf{d}^\top\mathbf{w} \] \[ D = \mathbf{Y}_0^\top V\mathbf{Y}_0 + \varepsilon\mathbf{I}_J,\qquad \mathbf{d} = \mathbf{Y}_0^\top V\,\mathbf{y}_1 \]

Estimated Weight Vector

\[ \mathbf{w}^{\text{SCM}} = \begin{pmatrix}w_1\\\vdots\\w_J\end{pmatrix},\quad w_j\geq 0,\;\textstyle\sum w_j=1 \]

Solved via SLSQP / quadprog. Weights are sparse and non-negative — no extrapolation.

Bias Bound

\[ \text{Bias} \;\propto\; \mathcal{O}\!\left(T_0^{-1/2}\right) \]

Bias shrinks with more pre-treatment periods. Fails when treated unit lies outside the convex hull of donors.

Augmented SCM — Ridge ASCM

Ben-Michael, Feller & Rothstein (2021)

Ridge Objective — Adds L2 Penalty to Spread Weights

\[ \mathbf{w}^{\text{Ridge}} \;=\; \arg\min_{\mathbf{w},\,\sum w_j=1} \; \underbrace{\|\mathbf{y}_1 - \mathbf{Y}_0\,\mathbf{w}\|_2^2}_{\text{pre-treatment fit}} \;+\; \underbrace{\lambda\,\|\mathbf{w}\|_2^2}_{\text{ridge penalty}} \]

Closed-Form KKT System

\[ \underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}\mathbf{Y}_0^\top\mathbf{Y}_0+\lambda\mathbf{I}_J & \mathbf{1} \\ \mathbf{1}^\top & 0\end{pmatrix}}_{(J{+}1)\times(J{+}1)} \begin{pmatrix}\mathbf{w}\\\nu\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix}\mathbf{Y}_0^\top\mathbf{y}_1 \\ 1\end{pmatrix} \] \[ \mathbf{w}^{\text{Ridge}} = \bigl(\mathbf{Y}_0^\top\mathbf{Y}_0+\lambda\mathbf{I}_J\bigr)^{-1}\!\bigl(\mathbf{Y}_0^\top\mathbf{y}_1 + \nu\mathbf{1}\bigr) \]

Augmented (Bias-Corrected) Estimator

\[ \hat{\tau}_t^{\text{ASCM}} = \underbrace{\bigl(Y_{1t} - \mathbf{Y}_{0t}^\top\mathbf{w}^{\text{Ridge}}\bigr)}_{\text{re-weighted difference}} + \underbrace{\bigl(\hat\mu_{1t} - \hat{\boldsymbol{\mu}}_{0t}^\top\mathbf{w}^{\text{Ridge}}\bigr)}_{\text{bias correction}} \]

Effect of \(\lambda\)

\(\lambda\to 0\): OLS weights (unconstrained)
\(\lambda\to\infty\): equal weights \((1/J)\)
\(\lambda\) chosen by cross-validation on held-out pre-treatment periods

Key: Allows negative weights (extrapolation). The bias-correction term \((\hat\mu_{1t}-\hat{\boldsymbol\mu}_{0t}^\top\mathbf{w})\) handles remaining imbalance via an outcome model. Weights sum to 1 but \(w_j\) can be \(< 0\).

SCM with Multiple Outcomes (SCM-MO)

Tian, Lee & Panchenko (2024)

Key Idea: Stack K Outcomes into One Joint Optimization

\[ \mathbf{Z}_1 = \begin{pmatrix}\mathbf{Z}_{1,1}\\\mathbf{Z}_{1,2}\\\vdots\\\mathbf{Z}_{1,K}\end{pmatrix}\!\in\mathbb{R}^{KT_0}, \quad \mathbf{Z}_0 = \begin{pmatrix}\mathbf{Z}_{0,1}\\\mathbf{Z}_{0,2}\\\vdots\\\mathbf{Z}_{0,K}\end{pmatrix}\!\in\mathbb{R}^{KT_0\times J}, \quad V = \bigoplus_{k=1}^K V_k \in\mathbb{R}^{KT_0\times KT_0} \]

Joint Optimization (Same Simplex Constraint)

\[ \mathbf{w}^{\text{MO}} = \arg\min_{\mathbf{w}\in\mathcal{W}} \sum_{k=1}^K (\mathbf{Z}_{1,k}-\mathbf{Z}_{0,k}\mathbf{w})^\top V_k(\mathbf{Z}_{1,k}-\mathbf{Z}_{0,k}\mathbf{w}) \] \[ = \arg\min_{\mathbf{w}\in\mathcal{W}} (\mathbf{Z}_1-\mathbf{Z}_0\mathbf{w})^\top V(\mathbf{Z}_1-\mathbf{Z}_0\mathbf{w}) \]

QP System

\[ D = \mathbf{Z}_0 V \mathbf{Z}_0^\top + \varepsilon\mathbf{I}_J,\quad \mathbf{d} = \mathbf{Z}_0 V\,\mathbf{Z}_1 \] \[ \min_{\mathbf{w}}\tfrac{1}{2}\mathbf{w}^\top D\mathbf{w} - \mathbf{d}^\top\mathbf{w},\quad \mathbf{1}^\top\mathbf{w}=1,\;\mathbf{w}\geq\mathbf{0} \]

Satellite Application

K = 8 bands (B2, B3, B4, B8, B11, B12, NDVI, NDWI)
One weight vector \(\mathbf{w}\in\mathbb{R}^5\) fits all 8 bands jointly.

Experiment 1

T₀ = 1, K = 8 → 8 matching cells
Single-period cross-band matching

Experiment 2

T₀ = 9, K = 8 → up to 72 matching cells
Full temporal + spectral matching

\[\text{Bias}\;\propto\;\mathcal{O}\!\left(T_0^{-1/2}\right)+\mathcal{O}\!\left(K^{-1/2}\right)\]

More outcomes AND more periods both reduce bias — powerful when \(T_0\) is small.
